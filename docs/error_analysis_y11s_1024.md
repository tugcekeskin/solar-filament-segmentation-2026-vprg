## Implications (ranked by estimated PQ upper-bound gain; not additive)

1. Mask quality (oracle masks on already-detected instances): +0.209 PQ upper bound; SQ is only 0.631 and inter-annotator SQ is also 0.631, so only part of this is reachable (annotator noise). 290 near-miss FN and 292 near-miss FP (IoU 0.3-0.5) point to shape/boundary errors: higher resolution/thin-structure handling, boundary-aware loss, mask refinement.
2. Near-miss conversion: turning the 290 near-miss FNs (and their 290 FPs) into TPs at 0.55 gives +0.109 PQ; same remedies as mask quality (they overlap with the item above, not additive).
3. Detection recall: 369 FNs (52%) are never proposed even at conf 0.01 (median area 673 px; see 3a/3b for where). Recovering them needs a better detector (larger imgsz, more data/augmentation, longer training); gain shown assumes SQ-level IoU for all.
4. Threshold/ranking recovery: 342 of 711 FNs have a raw instance with IoU>0.5 (median raw score 0.114); upper bound +0.091 PQ ignoring the FPs admitted at lower thresholds. Worth trying: per-image adaptive threshold, rescoring/learned confidence, keeping low-score instances when the image has few detections.
5. Unsupported FPs: 129 FP pairs on multi-view stems are hit by no annotator at all; removing them would give +0.016 PQ. The other 214 FP pairs on multi-view stems are filaments some annotator marked: they are annotator disagreement, not model error, and cannot be fixed beyond choosing which annotator style to follow (e.g. favour high-consensus structures: precision vs. support, see section 4).
6. Fragmentation: 33 GT instances covered by >=2 non-hitting preds (65 FP); union of fragments would hit 7 of them; fixing gives up to +0.016 PQ. Merging 43 preds spans >=2 GT (86 FN involved). Try mask-union / NMS-by-containment post-processing.
7. Ceiling: the inter-annotator PQ is 0.337 (SQ 0.631) while the model scores 0.358; multi-annotator stems score 0.371 vs single-annotator 0.340. Gains from further tuning are bounded by label noise; prioritise changes that are cheap and robust.
