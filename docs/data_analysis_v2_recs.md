# Data and error analysis v2: what to change in training (y11m_1536_flip_ref, val PQ 0.4675)

Hand-written findings and recommendations. All numbers come from the auto-generated tables below
(`scripts/analyze_data_v2.py`, ~30 s on 16 CPU workers, read-only on data/ and artifacts/). Val GT is used for
reporting only; nothing here was tuned on it. Expected impacts are estimates (hypotheses to test with an ablation,
not measurements). Figures: `docs/figs_v2/`.

## Executive summary

1. The images are geometrically canonical: disk radius 908.1 +- 1.9 px (CV 0.21%), centre offset 3.0 px mean / 15 px max, train and test identical. Neither multi-scale nor stronger scale/translate is justified by geometry; the 0.3 scale and 0.05 translate already cover about 100x the real variation.
2. Photometry is also nearly constant and there is no train/test shift (all KS <= 0.10, critical 0.115; Cohen d <= 0.16). Disk brightness CV is 2.1%, so hsv_v 0.3 (x0.7-1.3) is about 14x the natural spread. Only the sky halo level differs (bright halo in 24% of test vs 16% of train frames), and it has no effect on val recall/precision (0.640 vs 0.644 / 0.767 vs 0.707).
3. The dominant ceiling is annotator disagreement. Pairwise annotator-vs-annotator pooled PQ is 0.340. In multi-annotator val stems 68.8% of FN (194/282) were marked by no other annotator, and 66.7% of FP pairs (160/240) are predictions that another annotator did mark. Removing exactly those from the count would give PQ 0.5317 instead of 0.4675 (report-only counterfactual): the model-fixable headroom is about +0.06, and the realistic gain from any one training change is in the +0.005 to +0.015 range.
4. What is fixable: (a) mask quality, 37.7% of FP and 27.8% of FN are IoU 0.3-0.5 errors, and 154 of 211 shape-FP are undersized predictions (area < 0.8 x GT); (b) weak-contrast filaments, 86% of FN (473/551) have contrast d' < 2 (recall 0.55 there vs 0.84 for d' >= 2); (c) small filaments (recall 0.45 in the smallest area quintile vs 0.66-0.73 elsewhere); (d) score ranking, 35.6% of FN (196) have a raw proposal with IoU > 0.5 and score 0.10-0.25, but those bands hit only 38-41% of the time against a break-even of 0.34, so a lower conf gains almost nothing.
5. Orientation is not isotropic once latitude is considered: north-hemisphere filaments average -38.7 deg tilt, south +38.4 deg (an odd function of latitude). flipud preserves this rule; fliplr, rot180 and rot90 break it.

## Answers to the six questions

**Q1 Geometry/scale.** R = 908.1 +- 1.9 px train (902-914), 908.1 +- 2.1 test (905-914); R/mean p1-p99 = 0.998-1.006 (train), 0.997-1.007 (test); 2022 is +0.3%. Centre offset from the frame centre: mean 3.0 px, p95 4.4, max 13.6 (train) / 15.5 (test), i.e. below 1.7% of R. The disk has 105 px margin to the frame, so any rotation about the centre keeps it inside. Only 0.9% of annotation centroids are at r/R > 0.9 and one at > 0.95 (limb essentially unannotated). The only real scale variation is object size: area p5/p50/p95 = 369/1365/7341 px, width 5.5/9.1/18.8 px, spine length 41/111/387 px. Conclusion: multi-scale is not justified as a domain-shift fix (train = test geometry); its only role would be object-size regularisation, which scale already provides. translate=0.05 (+-102 px at 2048) is 7x the maximum real offset: adequate, even generous. Larger scale aug is not justified; if anything 0.3 -> 0.15-0.2 is worth an ablation.

**Q2 Orientation.** Pooled over the disk the axial tilt is almost isotropic (axial resultant 0.065; horizontal/diagonal/vertical 25.0/54.9/20.1% vs 25/50/25 uniform). It is strongly latitude dependent: north hemisphere mean tilt -38.7 deg (resultant 0.32; only 30.0% positive tilt), south +38.4 deg (0.31; 69.4% positive). Near the poles (|lat| > 0.6) filaments are horizontal (47% of the south and 43% of the north band within +-22.5 deg vs 21-25% elsewhere) and are ~20% longer than average (177 vs 147 px). Relative to the radial direction filaments are tangential-ish (angle to radial 46 -> 57 deg from centre to r/R > 0.9; 36% -> 53% tangential). Recall by orientation is flat (horizontal 0.603 / diagonal 0.664 / vertical 0.636), so the model does not underperform on rare orientations. Rotation by arbitrary angles is label-valid (class/chirality ignored) but breaks the odd-in-latitude rule: flipud keeps it (lat -> -lat, tilt -> -tilt), fliplr breaks it (tilt -> -tilt at the same latitude), rot180 breaks it (lat -> -lat, tilt unchanged), rot90 breaks it and the latitude-length relation. Large rotations also bring ultralytics' grey 114 fill into frames whose real corners are 0 (sky 29): an unseen-at-test texture. Do not use degrees up to 180/rot90 without an ablation; the data do not show an orientation recall gap that rotation would fix.

**Q3 Photometry.** Disk mean 126.6 +- 2.7 (CV 2.1%), p1-p99 multiplicative range 0.94-1.05; limb darkening r0.75-0.9 / centre 0.866 +- 0.025, r0.9-0.97 0.730 +- 0.030; per site the means span 124.0 (Th) to 127.9 (Bh) and the limb ratio 0.84 (Th) to 0.88 (Bh). Train vs test: Cohen d <= 0.16 and KS <= 0.10 for every metric (largest KS: limb ratio r.9-.97, 0.101, below the 0.115 critical value); site mix and year mix match (test has more 2021-22 frames: 11.7% vs 5.7%, with +0.3% larger disk radius in 2022). Sharpness varies a lot across frames (CV 44%, seeing) but equally in train and test. Filament contrast (ring minus inside) median 14.5 grey levels = 10.8% relative, d' median 1.63; weakest on Th/Bh (d' 1.50/1.57). Recommendation: hsv_v 0.3 is far wider than the real brightness spread and, being a pure gain, does not change relative contrast (the quantity that limits recall), so it is wasted; 0.1-0.15 is enough. A CLAHE or flat-field normalisation is not needed for domain shift; it could only help weak-contrast recall and must then be applied identically at train and test.

**Q4 Instances/annotators.** 7.1 annotations/view (median 7, p95 15, max 26). Median area 1365 px, width 9.1 px, spine length 111 px, solidity 0.60, bbox fill 0.26, sinuosity 0.95. At imgsz 1536 only 4.2% have width < 4 px, 2.2% have area < 300 px, none < 100 px. Crowding is low: 79.6% have no neighbour box within 30 px; median nearest-neighbour centroid distance 229 px; 5 within-view overlapping pairs total. Annotator style: 37 IDs, count per image relative to the stem mean ranges 0.82 (010403) to 1.24 (010402), std 0.12; median area per annotator 970 (020101) to 2315 (010203); share of own instances that no other annotator marked: 22-26% (0501xx, 010403) to 49-51% (010402, 010101); overall 37.2% in multi-annotator stems (43.7% with 2 annotators, 32.8% with 3). Agreement between annotators rises with size (37.8% -> 61.3% matched across area quintiles) and contrast (24% for d' < 0.5, 52% for 1-2, 81% for d' > 3). Annotator pairs PQ pooled 0.340 (TP 2144 / FP 1709 / FN 2000). Model val FN/FP: 68.8% of FN in multi-annotator stems and 66.7% of FP pairs are pure annotator disagreement (see 5.4); 269 of the 551 FN are in single-annotator stems where this cannot be judged.

**Q5 Model errors.** TP/FP/FN 994/393/551 reconciled exactly. By best IoU: FN 328 (59.5%) no overlap / 70 (12.7%) IoU<=0.3 / 153 (27.8%) IoU 0.3-0.5; FP 169 (43.0%) / 76 (19.3%) / 148 (37.7%). FN causes: A) raw proposal with IoU>0.5 and score >= conf lost in post-processing 29 (5.3%); B) raw IoU>0.5 but score < 0.25 196 (35.6%, median score 0.178); C) best raw IoU 0.3-0.5 162 (29.4%); D) no raw proposal IoU>0.3 at score >= 0.10: 164 (29.8%) are true detection misses. Raw hit rate by score band: 0.38 (0.10-0.15), 0.41, 0.41 (0.20-0.25), 0.46 (0.25-0.35), 0.60, 0.83 (> 0.5) vs break-even 0.34 (and the measured rates are optimistic because raw duplicates are not removed): lowering conf buys at most a rounding error. Breakdowns (recall): area quintile 0.453/0.658/0.692/0.728/0.686 (precision 0.635 -> 0.788); width quintile 0.550/0.663/0.673/0.631/0.699; contrast d' <0.5 0.10, 0.5-1 0.34, 1-2 0.60, 2-3 0.83, >3 0.88; r/R <0.35 0.677, 0.35-0.6 0.654, 0.6-0.8 0.629, 0.8-0.92 0.613 (precision 0.74 -> 0.61); latitude N>0.5 0.515 (n=99) vs 0.63-0.70 elsewhere; site 0.609 (Mh) - 0.676 (Ch); crowded filaments 0.562 (1-2 neighbours) vs 0.666 (isolated); by annotator support 0 / 1 / 2: 0.386 / 0.842 (>=1). Fragmentation: 28 GT split into >=2 preds (27 FN = 4.9% of FN; fragment union would reach IoU>0.5 for 9); merges: 26 preds cover >=2 GT (1.9% of preds, 18 FP). 189 FP (48.1%) lie >= 50% inside a GT without reaching IoU 0.5 and 109 FN (19.8%) are >=50% covered; of the non-hit preds overlapping a GT, 106 are < 0.5 x GT area and 48 are 0.5-0.8 x (total undersized 154 of 211), only 22 are > 2 x. Site, year and halo show no consistent effect.

**Q6 Copy-paste.** Filaments are isolated (79.6% no neighbour within 30 px), 57.9% have d' >= 1.5 (clearly visible dark objects), median solidity 0.60 and bbox fill 0.26 (thin curved objects; the mask, not the box, must be pasted), 9.4% are narrower than 6 px (hard-edged seams matter). The local background is textured (ring std 7.8 grey levels, comparable to half the 14.5 grey-level contrast), and pasted pixels bring their original background texture only inside the mask, so seams are visible but plausible. Default `copy_paste_mode='flip'` pastes the horizontally mirrored instances of the same image: mirrored pixels land on existing GT in 2.7% of mask pixels on average (5.5% of views > 10%) and off-disk in 0.02%, so it is mechanically safe because the disk is centred within 3 px. It adds roughly +7 filaments per view (doubling a 0.5% mask coverage), mirrored at the same latitude with tilt sign reversed (the same hemisphere-rule violation as fliplr). Suitable, moderately; the added samples copy annotator-idiosyncratic labels as well (37% unsupported).

## Ranked recommendations (<= 8)

Expected impact is the guess of the PQ change on val; risk is the main way it can backfire. Run each as a single-factor ablation against y11m_1536_flip_ref (different seeds vary by about +-0.005, so only changes >= 0.01 should be trusted; use val_raw caches and the same calibrated post-processing).

1. **Emphasise mask quality: `box=10.0` (the segmentation loss is scaled by `box` in ultralytics v8SegmentationLoss, so this raises box and mask loss together against cls/dfl).** Rationale: 37.7% of FP and 27.8% of FN are IoU 0.3-0.5 shape errors; 154/211 shape-FP are undersized masks; SQ is only 0.690. Expected +0.005 to +0.015. Risk: shifts score calibration (re-run `calibrate.py`), may hurt box-level recall of small filaments; try 10.0 before 12.5.
2. **Higher resolution for small/thin filaments: `imgsz=1792` (and predict at 1792), batch reduced to fit.** Rationale: smallest area quintile recall 0.45, width Q1 recall 0.55, 4.2% widths < 4 px at 1536, 20% < 7 px at 2048; at 1792 widths scale from 0.75 to 0.875. Expected +0.005 to +0.01, partially bounded by annotator agreement of only 38% in that quintile. Risk: ~36% more compute/memory; if the GPU job is the limit use `imgsz=1664`.
3. **Consensus label filter in 3-annotator stems (data prep, not an ultralytics arg): drop GT instances that no other annotator marked (~33% of 3006 instances, ~990), keep everything in 1- and 2-annotator stems.** Rationale: the metric scores each prediction once per annotator view; an instance supported by 1 of 3 views gives hit rate 0.33 < break-even 0.34, so predicting it is net negative; support 0 is idiosyncratic (010101 and 010402 have 49-51% unsupported). Only 80 of the 240 multi-annotator FP pairs are unsupported by every annotator, so the gain is capped (~+0.01). Expected +0.003 to +0.01. Risk: removes true small filaments; make the filter symmetric across annotators.
4. **Reduce photometric and geometric over-augmentation: `hsv_v=0.12, scale=0.2, translate=0.02`.** Rationale: real brightness spread is 2.1% CV (hsv_v 0.3 is x0.7-1.3), disk radius spread 0.2% and offsets <= 15 px; test geometry/photometry equals train, so these augs only add train/test mismatch and slow convergence. Expected 0 to +0.01. Risk: lower regularisation on 565 train stems; scale lower than 0.15 not advised (object sizes vary 20x).
5. **Keep the hemisphere-tilt prior: `fliplr=0.0, flipud=0.5` (and use only v-flip TTA).** Rationale: tilt is odd in latitude (N -38.7 deg, S +38.4 deg); flipud preserves it, fliplr breaks it. Expected -0.005 to +0.01 (unclear: halves effective flip diversity). Risk: loses a free 2x augmentation; the cached refs use h-flip TTA so TTA must be re-run. Worth testing only after 1-4.
6. **`copy_paste=0.2, copy_paste_mode='flip'`.** Rationale: filaments are isolated, mirror lands on existing GT in only 2.7% of pixels and never off the disk; helps instance density and crowded-case recall (0.56 with neighbours). Expected 0 to +0.01. Risk: seams on 9% thin filaments, copies idiosyncratic labels, same tilt-rule violation as fliplr, may raise FP (current FP precision 0.71).
7. **Post-processing: merge fragments (cluster preds that lie >= 50% inside the same union and re-test IoU of the union), keep `conf=0.25`.** Rationale: 27 FN come from >= 2 fragments, union reaches IoU > 0.5 in 9; the marginal-precision table (0.38-0.41 hit rate below conf vs 0.34 break-even, optimistic) says conf is already near optimum. Expected +0.002 to +0.004. Risk: wrong merges (26 merges already, 18 FP).
8. **Contrast normalisation experiment (pre-processing applied to train and test identically): flat-field by dividing by a large Gaussian (sigma ~60 px at 2048) then mild CLAHE (clip 2, 8x8 tiles).** Rationale: 86% of FN have d' < 2; recall 0.55 vs 0.84; limb darkening 0.73-0.87 makes relative contrast vary radially (recall 0.68 centre -> 0.61 near the limb). Expected 0 to +0.015, high variance. Risk: amplifies granulation (ring std 7.8) and shifts all learned features; requires rebuilding data/yolo and the predict pipeline.

**Explicitly not recommended:** `multi_scale` (R spread 0.2%; if tried, <= 0.1 only), `degrees` up to 180 or rot90 (breaks the latitude rule, grey corner fill), `shear`/`perspective` (no photometric or geometric shift to cover), `erasing` and `auto_augment` (classification-only in ultralytics 8.4; they are no-ops for segmentation), and lowering `conf` (marginal precision near break-even).
